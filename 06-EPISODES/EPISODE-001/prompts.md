Lighting state blocks (append exactly one per image; see `visual-style.md` §6):
- `GOLD`: late-August golden hour, low warm sun raking across the scene, long soft shadows, honey-gold light on stone and brass, cool blue-violet shade, fine dust glowing in the air
- `DAY`: clear late-summer day, soft high sunlight, crisp cool shadows, clean mountain air, gentle sky-blue bounce light
- `OVERCAST`: flat overcast grey sky, soft shadowless light, muted desaturated colors, damp air, wet-looking stone
- `DAWN`: early dawn, pale peach sky low on the horizon, cool blue shadows, thin mist lying in the valley, complete stillness, lamps still glowing faintly
- `DUSK`: blue-hour dusk, deep blue sky with a last thin warm glow along the mountain ridge, lamps just switched on, cool shadows with warm pools of lamplight
- `LAMP`: evening interior lit only by warm amber tungsten lamps, soft pools of light with deep gentle falloff into shadow, intimate and cozy
- `MIDNIGHT`: midnight, cool moonlit midnight-blue ambient light, deep soft shadows with visible detail, a single warm amber practical light for contrast, faint haze
