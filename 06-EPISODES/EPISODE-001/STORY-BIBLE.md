## 5. Fixed storytelling rules (from the prompt book)

1. Hook the viewer in the first 5–10 seconds.
2. Each episode needs a clear goal and stakes, escalating conflict, a turning point and climax, and a real emotional
   payoff.
3. Serialized episodes pay off the previous cliffhanger early and do not spend the whole episode delaying the answer.
4. Every cliffhanger is concrete, imminent, and earned by 1–3 fair clues planted earlier.
5. The final image or question must be understandable without an explanatory paragraph.
6. Banned: vague endings, arbitrary cut-offs, fake deaths, dream resets, and twists with no setup.
7. All content stays within the boundaries in `channel-guide.md` §3.
