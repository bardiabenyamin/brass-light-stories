### S01 — COLD OPEN — PLATFORM CLOCK / OFFICE — MONDAY, MIDNIGHT  [0:00–0:16]

A station clock in the dark: the minute hand glides onto twelve. The small stone station under a white moon. Inside,
an empty bottom shelf behind a brass grille, and a mustard-yellow umbrella glowing into being on the green baize.

> WREN (V.O.) *(close, unhurried)*: In Larkspur, things get lost the day before they go missing.

TITLE: **THE EARLY SHELF**  [0:16–0:20]

### S02 — PLATFORM — MONDAY, 6:14 PM — golden hour  [0:20–1:05]

The two-car railcar slides out of the tunnel into honey light. WREN (14, burnt-orange jacket, bob) steps down with one
bag and looks up at the station and the round clock.

> WREN (V.O.): The summer my parents needed to "sort some things out," they put me on a train to the end of the line.
> WREN (V.O.): Larkspur Junction. One station, one clock, and one train a day.

IDA (71, silver bun, bottle-green apron, brass key on a red cord) waits, hands folded into her apron.

> WREN (V.O.): Grandma met me on the platform like she was collecting a parcel.
> IDA *(dry, appraising)*: You have your mother's chin.
> WREN (V.O.): She didn't hug me. Grandma wasn't a hugger.

On the long bench, ABE (old railwayman, flat cap, red lantern at his feet) scratches LEDGER, a grey tabby, behind the
ears. THEO (15, copper curls, round glasses) compares his pocket watch to the station clock and frowns.

> WREN (V.O.): There was an old man on the bench, feeding a cat that clearly belonged to no one, and a boy checking his
> pocket watch against the station clock like one of them was lying.

### S03 — LOST-AND-FOUND OFFICE — MONDAY EVENING — lamplight  [1:05–1:45]

Ida leads Wren in. Shelves of forgotten things, sorted by color. The green-shaded desk lamp. A big green ledger.

> IDA: Everything lost in Larkspur ends up here. Sooner or later.
> WREN (V.O.): Hats. Gloves. A violin case. Every one of them waiting for somebody to remember.
> WREN (V.O.): Grandma wrote each one down in a big green ledger. Some of the lines had red thread stitched right
> through them.
> WREN: What's that for?
> IDA: Mending.

The bottom shelf behind its brass grille, empty, lined with green baize.

> WREN: And the bottom shelf?
> IDA: Is mine. Don't touch it.

Ida's hand closes around the brass key at her chest. *(K6)*

> WREN (V.O.): Grandma had exactly one rule. Naturally, I broke it the first night.

### S04 — STATION-HOUSE KITCHEN — MONDAY NIGHT — lamplight  [1:45–2:10]

Soup at the worn table under the pendant lamp. Through the small interior window, the back of the clock face glows.

> IDA *(absently)*: Eat your soup, Nora.
> WREN *(quiet)*: It's Wren, Grandma.
> IDA *(a beat; covering)*: Wren. I know that.
> WREN (V.O.): Nora is my mom. Grandma had never mixed us up before.  *(K3)*

### S05 — KITCHEN → OFFICE — MONDAY, MIDNIGHT  [2:10–2:55]

Wren alone at the kitchen's interior window, the clock's glowing disc beyond the glass. She lifts the tiny brass
whistle and plays four soft bars: Mom's song (MUSIC: `motif_mom`).  *(K5)*

> WREN (V.O.): I couldn't sleep, so I did what I always do. I played Mom's song on her whistle.
> WREN (V.O.): Then the station clock struck twelve, and the light under the office door turned gold.

Wren peeks around the office door frame, lit amber from below. The umbrella rests on the glowing shelf. Close on her
awe; her hand finds the whistle.

> WREN (V.O.): The bottom shelf had been empty when I went to bed.
> WREN (V.O.): Now there was an umbrella on it. Yellow. Mended near the tip. A little brass bird on the strap.

Behind her, in the dark hall doorway, Ida stands watching, her hand closing on her key.

> WREN (V.O.): I didn't know someone was watching me watch it.

### S06 — PLATFORM → OFFICE — TUESDAY, 6:14 PM — golden hour, the platform damp after rain  [2:55–3:35]

A WOMAN IN A CAMEL COAT (seen from behind) sits on the bench, a furled yellow umbrella across her knees.

> WREN (V.O.): It rained all Tuesday afternoon. At six-fourteen the sky cleared, the train came in, and a woman in a
> camel coat sat down on the bench with a yellow umbrella.
> WREN (V.O.): Mended near the tip. A little brass bird on the strap.

The railcar pulls away into the tunnel. In the foreground, the umbrella lies alone on the wet bench.

> WREN (V.O.): When the train left, she was on it. The umbrella wasn't.
> WREN (V.O.): I ran to the office.

The bottom shelf in evening light: the yellow umbrella **fades away**, and the shelf is empty.  *(R2)*

> WREN (V.O.): The bottom shelf was empty again. Like it had never been there at all.

### S07 — OFFICE — TUESDAY EVENING — lamplight  [3:35–4:05]

Theo comes in holding the dripping yellow umbrella.

> THEO *(deadpan)*: Someone left this on the bench.
> WREN (V.O.): Theo. He keeps the station clock running, and apparently he hands in umbrellas.
> THEO: Clock gained a minute again last night. No reason. And don't go up the tower, by the way. The stairs are
> rotten.  *(K2, K4)*

Insert: the mended tear, the brass sparrow charm.

> WREN (V.O.): Same tear. Same bird. Same umbrella.

### S08 — OFFICE — TUESDAY EVENING — lamplight  [4:05–4:40]

Wren sets the umbrella on the counter between them. Ida doesn't look surprised.

> WREN: This was on your shelf last night. Before she lost it.
> IDA: I know. I saw you.
> WREN: What is it?
> IDA *(quiet, final)*: The shelf shows what Larkspur will lose tomorrow. We watch. We write it down. And we never,
> ever give anything back early.
> WREN: Why not?
> IDA: Promise me, Wren.
> WREN *(a beat)*: I promise.
> WREN (V.O.): I meant it. For about six hours.

### S09 — OFFICE — TUESDAY, MIDNIGHT  [4:40–5:05]

The clock's minute hand reaches twelve again. The bottom shelf turns gold: something small and brass glows into being
on the baize.

> WREN (V.O.): At midnight, the clock struck twelve and the shelf turned gold again.
> WREN (V.O.): This time I recognized what was on it.
> WREN (V.O.): My mom's whistle.
> WREN (V.O.): Which was strange, because my mom's whistle was hanging around my neck.

Wren's two open hands, an identical brass whistle in each. Close on Wren: wonder turning to fear.

> WREN (V.O.): Tomorrow, I lose the only thing my mom ever gave me.

CUT TO BLACK. *(Cliffhanger: impossible evidence. Ep2 opens seconds later; see `cliffhanger-plan.md`.)*
