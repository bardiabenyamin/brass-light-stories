## 0. Cast-wide design rules

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Stylization | Original cinematic stylized 3D. Head and eyes about 10–15% larger than realistic; **realistic five-finger hands**; sculpted, appealing forms; tactile fabrics. No resemblance to any existing animated character or studio house style |
| One color anchor each | Wren **burnt orange** · Ida **bottle green** · Theo **faded indigo** · Crane **charcoal plus a white streak** · Abe **navy with brass buttons** · Ledger **grey tabby** |
| Reserved colors | **Mustard yellow** is used only for the Ep1 umbrella. **Red** is used only for the memory and love motif (Ida's cord and thread, Abe's lantern, the toy train, Nora's mitten). **Pale-gold glow** only on memory-objects; **amber glow** only on arrivals and returns. No character **wears or carries** these colors outside those uses. This applies to clothing and props; natural hair color (Theo's copper-red) is exempt |
| Family resemblance (Halloway) | Grey-green eyes · straight, slightly heavy dark brows · light freckles across the nose bridge (faded on Ida) |
| Family resemblance (Crane) | Pale grey eyes · long straight nose · narrow face. Subtle but deliberate: it is the visual half of clue K8 |
| Left and right | Always the **character's own** left or right. "Subject-left" appears on the **right of the image** in a front view |
| No on-character text | No badges, insignia, name tags, logos, or printed words on any clothing or prop |
